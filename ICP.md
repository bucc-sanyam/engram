# Knovis ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

## Who (Character Sketch)

**Sarah Chen, 26, Senior SDE at Meta, prepping for L7 interviews.**

Lives in San Francisco. 8 years of coding experience (started at FAANG at 18). Studies 1–2 hours daily on harder patterns and systems design problems. Hangs out on r/cscareerquestions, LeetCode Discuss, and Discord prep communities. High time-value (works 50hr weeks + side projects). Willing to pay $15–30/mo for tools that save her study time.

---

## Their Problem (The Pain)

**"I study a hard pattern for 6 hours, understand it deeply, then 1–2 weeks later I can't remember the approach when I see a similar problem in a real interview."**

Not a knowledge problem — she *learns* well. It's a **retention problem**. The pattern feels obvious mid-study, then vanishes. Watches her own solution on LeetCode, nods, moves on. Come interview day, blank.

---

## How They Solve It Today (The Status Quo)

1. **LeetCode Premium** ($160/yr) — practices problems, watches editorial, forgets them
2. **Anki** — tried to build flashcards, gave up after 10 cards (manual + boring)
3. **Notion** — keeps scattered notes about patterns, never reviews them
4. **Rereading solutions** — when she gets stuck on a similar problem, she googles the old solution and copy-pastes the logic

Basically: **no system for spaced recall.** She re-learns the same patterns 3–4 times per year. Frustrating. Expensive in terms of time.

---

## Why They'd Pay (The Outcome They Want)

**"Hit every interview question I've *already studied before* with confidence. No surprises. No 'oh god I knew this.'"**

Outcome-focused: not "I want flashcards" but **"I want to never forget a pattern I've already mastered."**

Willing to pay because:
- Saves ~5 hours/week on re-learning (time = money at her salary)
- Increases interview success rate (each level bump = $50k+ more comp)
- Tools that *actually work* (Anki doesn't work for her; Knovis AI auto-generates cards)

---

## Why Knovis Wins Their Money

| Competitor | Their model | Why Sarah leaves |
|---|---|---|
| **Anki** | Manual flashcards | "I'm too lazy to make 200 cards. I forget to review them." |
| **LeetCode/NeetCode** | Practice problems + editorial | "I practice, watch the solution, forget it. No recall system." |
| **Notion** | Note-taking + manual scheduling | "I have 50 scattered notes I never look at again." |

**Knovis:** AI auto-generates recall questions (no manual work), spaced-repetition scheduling (you don't have to remember to review), and 3D knowledge graph (you *see* what you know vs. what's fuzzy). The second-brain part = personalized, grounded in *her* learning journey, not generic.

---

## Red Flags (When NOT Our Customer)

- Hasn't already used LeetCode / studied at interview level (too junior)
- Wants to *learn* patterns for the first time (that's NeetCode's job; we help you *retain* what you learned)
- Only studies 5 minutes/day (too low volume for spaced rep to matter)
- Fully satisfied with Anki (rare, but they exist)

---

## Summary

**The Beachhead:** 26–35 year old software engineers at or above Senior level, prepping for big career moves (promotions, Big Tech interviews, side projects). They've already *invested* in learning. They're willing to pay to protect that investment (not lose it to forgetting).

**The Promise:** "You'll remember every pattern you've already mastered — no more re-learning."

**The Wedge:** Coding interview prep. (Expand to med school, law school, language learning, once the loop is proven.)
